Open Adventure: licences
========================

The game: Colossal Cave Adventure by Will Crowther and Don Woods (1976-
1995), forward-ported as Open Adventure by Eric S. Raymond and others,
https://gitlab.com/esr/open-adventure. The authors gave permission for
this release.

- The program and the cave (adventure.yaml, built into the program) are
  under the BSD 2-Clause licence (COPYING.txt).
- The guide (Guide) and ReadMe-OpenAdventure are upstream's, under
  CC-BY-4.0.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/, AMIGA_PORT.md, package/, tools/,
  tests/) are under the BSD 2-Clause licence, like the game; the GlowIcon
  art is The AmiCradle Team's own.
